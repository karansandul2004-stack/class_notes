Statistics

Statistics is the branch of mathematics that deals with collecting, organizing, analysing and presenting data.


Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive Statistics is the art of summarising past and present data to uncover patterns, trends and structure.
It doesn't predict or infer-it simply describes reality as it is, transforming raw data into clear, meaningful insights though
measures of center, spread and shape.
