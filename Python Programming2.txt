Normalization: Arrangement pf data
Normalization in DBMS is the process of organizing database tables to reduce data redundancy and improve data
integrity by structuring data logically, often by splitting large tables into smaller, related ones, to eliminate
inconsistencies (anomalies) during insert, update, and delete operations, following a set of rules known
as Normal Forms (like,1NF,2NF, 3NF,BCNF).

First Normal Form: 1NF
Second Normal Form: 2NF
Third Normal Form: 3NF
Boyce Codd Normal Form: BCNF

In a DBMS(Database Management System), a tuple is a single, complete record or row in table,
representing one instance of an entity, like one person or one product, with all its associated data values (attributes).
Column: Field
In a database, a field is a single, specific category of data (like "Name," "Age," or "Email")that forms a column in a table, defining 
the type of information stored, while a record (a row) contains the actual data for one entity across all its fields.
